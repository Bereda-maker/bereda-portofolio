## Meals & Nutrition Management — implementation complete

**Scope notes:** This completion covers the shipped manual-first feature. External FDC lookup is not activated without a server-only key; Open Food Facts import remains deferred pending license review; OCR and image upload remain unavailable until durable storage and reviewable OCR are configured. The planner intentionally uses only recorded inventory and confirmed nutrition data: it does not price hypothetical purchases or convert a fitness goal into a calorie/macro prescription. Allergies and restrictions are hard exclusions, preferences are soft ranking signals, and activity is used only by the optional cited adult EER estimate. No database migration was applied and no sample health, food, budget or meal records were created during development.

- [x] **Meals navigation and pages:** Added a standalone protected admin item named Meals & Nutrition with a Utensils icon and `/admin/meals` route. Provides Overview, My Foods, Daily Meal Planner, Nutrition Tracking, Body Profile, Meal History, Food Spending and Settings views. Meals remains independent of Personal Finance, Learning and other admin modules. Updated the route manifest for every page. Follows the existing Bereda admin design system with responsive UI, simple daily budget entry and no unnecessary charts.
- [x] **Food catalog and nutrition profiles:** Manual registration supports food name, category, quantity and explicit units, separate optional piece count and total weight, price/currency/price unit, preparation, availability, expiry, image reference and notes. Nutrition profiles support calories, macro- and micronutrients, per-100g, per-100ml or named-serving basis, explicit units for every known value, source, provenance/confidence and versioned manual corrections. Missing/null nutrient values remain unknown rather than zero. Piece count and weight are never interchangeable without explicit conversion data.
- [x] **Food-data lookup and label handling:** Manual label/reference entry is supported and source-attributed; the Ethiopian Food Composition Table 2025 is linked as a reference and is not bulk-copied. FDC lookup remains inactive without a real server-only API key; Open Food Facts import is deferred pending license review. OCR/scanning and image upload remain unavailable until durable storage and reviewable OCR are configured; future OCR values require manual review. Nutrition and cost calculations permit only explicitly supported serving/unit conversions.
- [x] **Food inventory and purchase history:** Tracks purchase lots, available/consumed/remaining quantities, explicit units, price/currency and expiry. Deduction occurs only after actual consumption is explicitly recorded; plans do not reserve or deduct stock. Manual inventory adjustments are append-only and idempotent, with purchase/consumption history retained. Expired, unavailable, allergy-conflicting, restriction-conflicting or insufficient-stock foods are excluded. The planner explicitly uses recorded current inventory only and does not assume that hypothetical foods can be purchased within the budget.
- [x] **Private body profile and safety:** Supports age, height, dated weight, optional biological sex, activity, fitness goal, preferences, allergies, restrictions and meal frequency. BMI is informational for adults only and BMI alone never determines targets or recommendations. Adult NASEM 2023 EER is an estimate with cited source, assumptions, recent-weight requirement and uncertainty; it is gated away for minors, under-19 profiles, pregnancy/lactation, eating-disorder concern, medical conditions or missing/unknown safety inputs. No prescriptive calorie/weight-loss targets or therapeutic advice are generated; individualized needs are referred to qualified professionals.
- [x] **Daily budget and independent meal planning:** Supports dated daily budget, currency (ETB default), meals count and optional notes, independent of Personal Finance. Deterministic decimal-safe selection uses recorded budget/prices, eligible stock, confirmed nutrition profiles, explicit unit/portion conversions, allergy/restriction filters, preference soft signals and meal frequency. Generates meal-type suggestions and displays cost and known nutrition totals, optional adult EER comparison, alternatives and explicit shortfall/reasoning. No validated macro targets or hypothetical purchase options are inferred. AI is not the calculation or safety-filter engine and cannot override saved facts.
- [x] **Plan changes, favorites and actual consumption:** Supports accepting plans, replacement/serving changes, adding/removing items, favorites and recording actual meals/deviations. Revisions create a new draft with recalculated known cost/nutrients; prior snapshots remain in history. Acceptance revalidates current availability, expiry, allergy/restriction compatibility and stock within a transaction, serializes competing acceptance actions, and supersedes a prior accepted plan. Plans never count as consumed; only explicit confirmation records intake and updates stock/nutrition/spending.
- [x] **Daily tracking and progress:** Separates planned and consumed meals; provides recorded energy/macros/fiber, optional hydration, actual spending and remaining budget. Supports dated weight/body measurements/notes. Weekly/monthly trends use averages and do not interpret daily weight change or promise outcomes. Historical records are retained.
- [x] **Food spending, Finance link and AI:** Reports actual daily/weekly/monthly spending, daily/meal averages, cost per calorie/protein gram where supported, frequently eaten foods, affordable protein sources and planned-versus-actual spending from saved records only. Finance links are off by default and require explicit setting plus per-lot confirmation; the UI distinguishes linked and unlinked purchase lots. Unique idempotent linkage limits each actual purchase lot to at most one Finance expense and preserves Finance history. AI is optional, consent-gated, one-turn and only receives selected saved data when the user explicitly opts in and has enabled sensitive-data consent. It is labeled as non-diagnostic/non-prescriptive and cannot invent availability, nutrition, prices or measurements. Sensitive data is excluded from ordinary logs and is not sent without consent.
- [x] **Secure architecture and verification:** Added normalized additive schema and migration, protected admin CRUD, strict server validation, relational integrity, decimal-safe calculations and immutable plan/consumption snapshots. Nutrition calculation is separated from AI. Development did not apply the migration to a shared database or create sample personal records. Verification includes manual nutrition/unit and money calculations, inventory and selection engine rules, validation, adult EER safety boundaries, stale-weight guards, hydration, acceptance routing/safety invariants, Learning/Finance/Life AI/Opportunities regressions, TypeScript checks, route manifest checks and production builds.
